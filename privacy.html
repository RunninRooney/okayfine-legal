Privacy Policy for Morse

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Morse ("the app") collects, why, and what your choices are. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The app is published by Okay Fine LLC ("we", "us"), which is the party responsible for the information described here.

The short version

  • We do not collect any data from you at all.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device. We do not have a copy of it.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, address, or phone number.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and nothing to buy in this version.
  • The app does not need a network connection, and does not send your information anywhere.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally: your level, your per-letter accuracy statistics, your awards, your settings, and your scores. This file lives in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Information we collect

None. This version of the app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no other software that gathers information about you or your device. We do not operate any server that receives data from the app, and the app does not create or store an advertising identifier or any similar identifier for you.

Permissions the app requests

  • Vibration. Used only to produce the tap feedback you feel while keying morse. It reads nothing and sends nothing.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning game. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children either. If you have any concern about a child's use of the app, you can contact us at the address below.

Your choices

  • Delete your data. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. Because we never receive a copy and hold no server-side record, there is nothing further for us to delete, and nothing for you to request.

Data retention

We do not operate servers that store your information. Locally stored progress remains on your device until you delete the app.

Future versions

We may later add advertising, an ad-free purchase, optional account sign-in, or optional gameplay statistics. None of those are present today. If any of them is added, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what it collects before or alongside the release that introduces it, the store listing's data disclosures will be updated to match, and any collection that is optional will be accompanied by a control in the app's own settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.
