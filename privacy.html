Privacy Policy for Morse

Last updated: 6 August 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Morse ("the app") collects, why, and what your choices are. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

The app is published by Okay Fine LLC ("we", "us"), which is the party responsible for the information described here.

The short version

  • Your game progress is stored on your device. We do not have a copy of it.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, address, or phone number.
  • We collect anonymised statistics about how the game is played, so we can improve it. You can turn this off in the app's settings.
  • The app shows ads. Our advertising partner collects some device information to do that.
  • If you buy anything, the payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally: your level, your per-letter accuracy statistics, your awards, your settings, and your scores. This file lives in the app's private storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Gameplay statistics

To understand how the game is actually played, and therefore how to improve it, the app records a small set of events about gameplay:

  • which game mode was started, how long it was played, and whether it was finished;
  • level completions, including the level number, whether it was completed without mistakes, and how many hints were used;
  • speed run scores and accuracy;
  • awards unlocked;
  • how long a play session lasted;
  • whether an advertisement was shown, and the result of a purchase attempt.

These events describe gameplay only. They do not contain your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or anything you type outside of the game's own morse practice. We use them in aggregate - for example, "how many players try Speed mode" - not to look at any individual.

How anonymous this is, precisely: these statistics are not linked to your name or to any account. They are, however, associated with a randomly generated identifier for your installation of the app, which is what allows two events from the same play session to be recognised as related. That makes the data pseudonymous rather than strictly anonymous. Deleting and reinstalling the app generates a new identifier.

You can switch this off at any time: Settings → Share play data. Turning it off stops collection; the game is fully playable either way, and we do not treat you differently for declining.

Information collected by our advertising partner

The app displays advertisements between levels. To serve and measure those ads, our advertising provider may collect and process:

  • a resettable advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android, or the Identifier for Advertisers on iOS, where you have permitted it);
  • your IP address, and a coarse location derived from it (typically country or region);
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language, and screen size;
  • information about ads shown to you, such as which ads were displayed, viewed, or tapped.

This data is handled by the advertising provider under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your advertising identifier or your IP address, and we do not build a profile of you.

Purchases

If you purchase the ad-free upgrade, the transaction is processed by the App Store or Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, so the app can unlock the feature. We never receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or account password.

Optional account sign-in

If the app offers sign-in and you choose to use it, we receive an account identifier supplied by that provider so your progress can be associated with your account. We do not receive your password. You can play the entire game without ever signing in.

Children

The app is a general-audience learning game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where the app is distributed to audiences that include children, advertising is configured to be non-personalised and age-appropriate, and no behavioural advertising identifier is used for those users. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Limit ad tracking. On iOS, decline the tracking permission prompt, or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, use Settings → Google → Ads to delete or opt out of your advertising ID.
  • Turn off gameplay statistics. Settings → Share play data.
  • Remove ads. Purchasing the ad-free upgrade stops ads, and therefore stops the associated data collection described above.
  • Delete your data. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. Because we do not hold a server-side copy, there is nothing further for us to delete unless you have signed in to an account, in which case you may email us to request deletion.

Data retention

We do not operate servers that store your personal information. Locally stored progress remains on your device until you delete the app. Data held by our advertising or payment partners is retained according to their own policies.
